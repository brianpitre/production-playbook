Introduction

Welcome to the Production Team

Welcome to the Production Team. Thank you for being part of the Production Team at The Life Church! As expressed in Psalm 92:12-15, we believe being planted in God's house and serving with others is one of the most important aspects of being a Christian.

Every weekend, thousands of people walk through the doors of The Life Church looking for something real. What they experience doesn't happen by accident. It happens because a skilled, committed production team shows up prepared, serves with excellence, and remains invisible in all the right ways.

That's the role you play. Production doesn't draw attention to itself. It creates the conditions for something greater to happen. When your work is done well, people encounter God without distraction. When it isn't, they notice the technology instead of the moment.

This playbook is your guide. It covers who we are, how we operate, what each position requires, and the specific steps you need to follow every single week. Read it carefully, refer back to it often, and bring this standard every time you serve.

Foundation

Vision, Purpose & Goal

Vision We are one church in many locations — dynamic, Spirit-filled and diverse — serving people, developing leaders and impacting generations.
Purpose Our purpose is to leverage every tool at our disposal — video, lighting, graphics, time-keeping, and more — to serve the vision of the church by creating a dynamic environment where people can have a genuine experience with God.
Goal Our goal is to consistently produce excellent services and events that are free from distraction, while continuously growing, equipping, and empowering the volunteers who make it happen.
Standards

Key Attributes of a Production Team Member

Technical skill matters, but it's secondary to character. These are the qualities that define every member of this team.

Detail-Oriented You notice what others miss. You catch problems before they become distractions and correct them without being asked.
Punctual You are at your position, ready, at your call time, not walking in at your call time. The team depends on you being in place and ready to go.
Available You show up for your scheduled services and are willing to serve for special events when needed. Reliability is part of the role.
Decisive You execute confidently with the things you've rehearsed and the things you haven't. When the plan changes, you adapt and move forward without hesitation.
Flexible Services change. Elements shift. You adapt quickly and calmly without frustration, and you communicate changes clearly to the rest of the team.
Patient You stay composed and attentive from the first moment to the last. Fatigue, stress, and long mornings don't show up in your work.
Teachable You receive feedback without defensiveness and actively look for ways to improve. Your best work is always in front of you, not behind you.
Team-First You serve the vision over personal preference. You communicate proactively, cover gaps, and never leave a teammate hanging.
How We Operate

Team Overview

The Life Church operates a two-model production structure across its locations. The Houston Levee location serves as the Broadcast Location. It originates the live service, running a full broadcast production that is distributed via Vitec (our video distribution system) to four Receiving Locations in real time.

Each of the four Receiving Locations has its own production team responsible for seamlessly integrating the incoming Vitec feed while managing local production elements — videos, slides, and any other on-site content.

This means the two locations operate similarly but carry different weights of responsibility. Houston Levee controls what other locations see. Receiving Locations ensure their local experience matches the broadcast location's quality and intention.

Broadcast Location — Houston Levee Originates the live program feed. Runs full production including all cameras, graphics, video playback, and the outgoing Vitec signal. The Broadcast Director monitors and manages the outbound feed.

Changes or updates in services are communicated regularly through Google Chat groups. The Broadcast Time Clock is viewable via a YouTube link.

Receiving Locations (4 locations) Receive the live Vitec feed and integrate it with local production. Manage videos, local slides, notes (LT) and attend the Team Touchdown before each service to align on flow and transitions.

Throughout this playbook, differences between Broadcast and Receiving responsibilities are noted clearly in each section.

Roles

Production Positions & Responsibilities

Some positions exist at multiple locations with location-specific scope; others exist only at Houston Levee. Multi-location positions are listed first.

ProPresenter Operator

The ProPresenter Operator runs ProPresenter for the service. The specific scope of this role varies by location — see the location callouts below for the responsibilities that apply at each location.

  • Select the Sunday service playlist and verify all slides and videos before rehearsal
  • Follow all songs during Worship Team rehearsal — know every verse, chorus, and bridge
  • Lead lyrics consistently early — never be on time or lag behind the Worship Team
  • Fire videos and advance slides precisely on cue
  • Communicate immediately if a slide or lyrics are missing, incorrect, or a video fails to load
  • Know the Clear All function and use it without hesitation if wrong content goes live
Broadcast Location — Houston Levee Handles lyrics, message notes, and lower-thirds titles & slides.
Receiving Locations — Memphis Same scope as Houston Levee, plus Time Clock operation.
Receiving Locations — DC Metro & Nashville Handles all slides and video playback, plus executing cue for message playback (the Vitec video stream plays through PVS — ProVideoServer — to ProPresenter). Also handles lighting cues and transitions.

Team Lead

The Team Lead is responsible for the precise exection of the service flow. This position is the bridge between the Production Team and 5 Star. Team Leads and 5 Stars from all locations remain in constant communication to ensure coordination between Production Team members, the Worship Team and anyone speaking from stage.

  • Carefully review service flow and note important transitions
  • Review message notes for cosistency with written notes submitted from speakers
  • Lead team touchdown prior to service – review service flow with team
  • Highlight key transitions with specific team positions
  • Call transitions for team execution when necessary
  • Communicate technical issues along with approximate resolution times
  • Contact Production Technician immediately in the case of any technical issues

PVP Operator

The PVP (ProVideoPlayer) Operator manages all motion background and environmental video content throughout the service. This position works in close coordination with the ProPresenter Operator and the Tech Director to ensure backgrounds are correct, transitions are clean, and every clip is cued and ready.

  • Select playlist and verify all cues are present, operational and in the correct order
  • Test playback quality, color, and audio levels for every cue before rehearsal
  • Confirm PVP output is routing correctly to the switcher, screens, etc
  • Coordinate with the ProPresenter Operator and Director on shared timing and transitions
  • Respond to Team Lead and Director cues in real time
Receiving Locations — Memphis Handles everything the same as Houston Levee, plus executing cue for message playback (the Vitec video stream plays through PVS — ProVideoServer — to PVP).

Lighting Director

The Lighting Director executes pre-programmed lighting cues in time with each service segment. This role does not program lighting — it runs the show. Tight cue execution shapes the room's energy at every transition: worship, message, MC moments, etc. all rely on the Lighting Director to fire the right look at the right time.

  • Verify the lighting console and lighting fixtures are online and all programmed cues load correctly
  • Review the service flow and cue list before rehearsal — know every cue and its trigger
  • Execute pre-programmed cues precisely during song progression and on segment transitions
  • Hold cues steady during performance; never busk or freelance lighting changes mid-segment
  • Communicate immediately if a cue fails to fire or a fixture goes down
  • Coordinate with the Team Lead and 5 Star on any timing adjustments during the service
Broadcast Location — Houston Levee Also executes Main Screen movements as indicated in the service flow.
Receiving Locations — Memphis Executes pre-programmed lighting cues only.

PVS Operator

The PVS (ProVideoServer) Operator manages the video server that receives the live Vitec broadcast feed from Houston Levee and routes it into the local production system (PVP and ProPresenter). This position is critical for receiving locations — every video that originates from the broadcast location, including the message, passes through PVS before it reaches the room.

  • Power on the PVS and confirm the Vitec feed is incoming, stable, and routed correctly
  • Verify all pre-loaded videos and message playback content are present, in order, and cued correctly
  • Test playback of every cue before rehearsal — confirm video and audio quality
  • Confirm PVS output is routing correctly to PVP and ProPresenter (verify with PVP and ProPresenter Operators)
  • Cue and fire message playback precisely on the Team Lead's call
  • Communicate immediately if the Vitec feed drops, lags, or shows quality issues
  • Coordinate with the PVP Operator, ProPresenter Operator, and Team Lead on shared timing and transitions

Director

The Director owns the visual presentation of the service. Working from the production booth, the Director calls every camera shot, communicates transitions to the Tech Director, and ensures what goes to screen is engaging, focused, and distraction-free. This role requires deep preparation. The Director should know the service better than anyone in the room.

  • Study the service flow and worship setlist in advance of each service
  • Call all camera shots clearly and confidently — by number and frame description when necessary
  • Communicate transitions, segment changes, and cues to the Tech Director and Camera Operators
  • Monitor pacing, framing, and composition on every shot going to program
  • Respond immediately to changes communicated by the 5 Star or other team members
  • Lead camera rehearsal during the Full Service Run-through
  • Attend Touchdown and confirm any last-minute changes
  • Coordinate with the Broadcast Director on transition timing and outbound signal quality
Receiving Location - Nashville This position also manages livestream events and streaming services on YouTube.

Tech Director

The Tech Director operates the video switcher, executing the Director's shot calls in real time. This role demands precision, fast reflexes, and total familiarity with the switcher layout. Every cut and transition is a live, unforgiving action — there are no retakes.

  • Verify all switcher inputs and outputs before rehearsal
  • Execute cuts exactly as called by the Director
  • Monitor all camera feeds and source inputs on the multiviewer continuously
  • Manage video playback switching between live cameras and pre-recorded content
  • Be prepared to go to a backup source immediately there is a camera issue or if a feed is lost
  • Communicate any signal issues to the Director right away

Camera Operator

Camera Operators capture the people and moments that make the service compelling on screen. This role demands technical precision — clean focus, smooth movements, proper framing — and the instinct to have the right shot ready before the Director calls it.

  • Check pan, tilt, zoom, and focus before rehearsal begins
  • Execute pan, tilt, zoom, and focus with smooth, deliberate movements
  • "Sell" shots to the Director — frame confidently and move steadily until called
  • Respond to Director's shot calls instantly and without hesitation
  • Study the setlist and service flow to anticipate movements and key moments
  • Communicate to the Director immediately if a shot is unavailable or a camera issue arises

Time Clock Operator

The Time Clock Operator manages service timing, keeping the production team informed of where the service stands against the planned flow at every moment. This role is critical to preventing overruns and ensuring smooth, on-time transitions between services.

  • Review all time segments in the service flow before the run-through
  • Align countdown segments to match the service flow sequence
  • Track actual vs. planned timing during run-through; document all discrepancies
  • Communicate timing information to the Team Lead and 5 Star proactively
  • Alert the Team Lead if any segment is running long or short
  • Adjust clock cues on the fly as the service evolves

Broadcast Director

The Broadcast Director is a Houston Levee-specific role responsible for the outgoing program feed distributed via Vitec to all four Receiving Locations. Working alongside the Director, the Broadcast Director ensures the feed is broadcast-quality, properly framed for a distributed audience, and technically stable at all times.

  • Review the full service flow with a broadcast lens — identify every Vitec transition point
  • Confirm Vitec distribution is online and feeding all Receiving Locations before service
  • Continuously monitor the outgoing program feed — not just in-room content
  • Coordinate with the Tech Director on transition timing for broadcast-appropriate cuts
  • Ensure all pre-recorded content is broadcast-quality before it airs
  • Communicate feed status to Receiving Locations if technical issues arise

Recording Engineer

The Recording Engineer captures all video feeds from the service for post-production use — including message editing and content distribution. This role requires meticulous attention to signal quality and disciplined file management after every service.

  • Set up all recording equipment and verify signal routing before call time
  • Insert and verify recording media (SSD) before service begins
  • Test record and verify audio and video quality before the run-through
  • Monitor recording levels and signal throughout the entire service
  • Properly label and back up all media immediately after service
  • Deliver edited content per agreed timeline
  • Manage livestream events and streaming services on YouTube

Production Assistant

The Production Assistant supports the broadcast production team across the entire service — from setup through teardown. This is a flexible, hands-on role that fills gaps, assists every position, and keeps the production environment running smoothly. The Production Assistant is the team's extra set of hands and eyes.

  • Arrive early to assist with power and setup of equipment as needed
  • Run errands between the control room, stage, and other locations as directed
  • Distribute and manage comms packs, batteries, and small production gear as needed
  • Relay messages between the Team Lead, 5 Star, and on-stage talent when comms are not in use
  • Be available throughout the service to respond to any team member's request
  • Respond to or assist with any issues that arise during services
  • Assist with teardown and storage of all equipment after the final service
Onboarding

Training Process

Training happens at each location during weekend services through a structured shadowing model. The timeline adjusts based on the individual's prior experience, but the progression does not skip steps. No one moves to a rostered position without completing the full training process and meeting with the Team Lead.

Broadcast Location — Houston Levee

PhaseFocusDescription
Weeks 1–2 I Do. You Watch. Observe the position in full. Take notes. Ask questions after the service.
Week 3 Location Visit Observe at a Receiving Location. Understand what they receive via Vitec and what they need from the broadcast location to do their job well.
Week 4 You Do. I Help. Take the position with the trainer alongside you. The Team Lead evaluates readiness and team fit at this stage.
Weeks 5–6 You Do. I Watch. Operate fully independently. Trainer is present but hands off entirely unless an issue arises.

Receiving Locations

PhaseFocusDescription
Weeks 1–2 I Do. You Watch. Observe the position in full. Understand the Vitec integration, local responsibilities, and Touchdown process.
Week 3 You Do. I Help. Take the position with the trainer alongside you. The Team Lead evaluates readiness and team fit.
Weeks 4–5 You Do. I Watch. Operate fully independently. Trainer is present but hands off.
Before training is considered complete, every team member must meet with the Team Lead for a brief evaluation conversation.

ProPresenter Operator Checklist

Preservice

Before Full Service Run-Through

  • Power on the ProPresenter computer

  • Click through every slide in the playlist — verify text, formatting, and media
  • Confirm all videos are present and will play correctly

  • Know the location of the Clear All button — use it immediately if wrong content goes live

During Full Service Run-Through

Goal: Know the content well enough that you are leading the congregation — not following them.

  • MC Segments — Advance announcement graphics on Director's or 5 Star's cue
  • Special Segments — Confirm timing for any custom slides or overlays
  • Message — Follow message notes; confirm all scripture slides are ready and in order

After Full Service Run-Through

  • Note and correct any slide errors before first service
  • Confirm any changes with Director or 5 Star
Touchdown
  • Note any last-minute slide additions or changes from 5 Star or Director
